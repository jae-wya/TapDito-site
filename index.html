Questions

Do my customers need an app?

No. iPhone XS and newer read NFC on their own, and so do most Android phones with NFC switched on. Every piece also has a QR code for phones without NFC.

What does 2 NFC tags mean?

The acrylic card gets two chips. Each one opens a different link, for example your Facebook page and your Instagram.

Is the design included?

Yes. Every product includes a custom design based on your branding. Templated verse keychains use our ready-made templates with your chosen verse.

Does it work through a phone case?

Thin plastic and silicone cases are fine. Thick wallet cases and cases with metal plates or magnets can block the signal.

How do I pay?

GCash, Maya, bank transfer, or cash on pickup. We send payment details with your design proof.
